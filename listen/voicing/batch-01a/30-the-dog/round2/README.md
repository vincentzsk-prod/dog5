## What the measurements say (read before listening)

1. **No oscillator, filter or drive lever moves the band split by more than about ±1.5 dB at equal level.** The spectrum is a stack of saws (1/n), so O2 up, O1 up, O2 at 0 st or −7,
   KEY TRK, CUTOFF, ENV AMT all trade a dB or less between bands (the sweeps are in the log). The honest "fuller" in E is small: +0.7 dB above 1.2 kHz, nothing below.
   What *does* change the feel is **width** (inner motion of the 60–400 Hz band: s.d. 0.99 dB in B, 1.47 in E; round 1's C measured 1.65) and the **level** in H.
2. **Wider, not C:** DETUNE 28 → 35 gives 14.8 cents (9.4 before, C 23.4); VINTAGE stays 40, so the per-card wander is unchanged (s.d. 1.2–1.9 cents, range −7 … +8).
   The 8 voices are 2.1 cents apart (B 1.3).
3. **The snap needs a caveat.** Round 1's "+4.0 dB" was the held note's peak against its *last 110 ms*; with the stack beating at 55 Hz that body level moves by ±2 dB with the beat
   phase. Against the *whole hold* B's snap is +0.5 dB, and on the eight chopped roots +0.9 dB (worst +0.1). The punch he likes is mostly spectral:
   1224 → 725 Hz. All of E, F, H keep or improve the steadier readings (+1.4 / +1.8 / +1.7 whole-hold; chop +0.8 / +0.7 / +0.7)
   and the brightness ratio (B 1.69, E 1.87, H 1.84); G, the edge, loses some (1.58).
   **By round 1's own literal reading (last 110 ms) E–H read +1.5 … +2.5, under the +3 line**, but B's +4.0 was beat-phase luck; I did not try to game it.
4. **Release** stays 54–64 ms to −60 dB, no FX on (no spring, no echo): the limit is 80.
5. **Drive routes** (ladders on the E base, equal level, below): **per-voice DRIVE** (FILTER+ knob 1, before the filter) adds inter-harmonic content (hash 7.7 → 10.6 % at 110) and takes
   1.1–1.9 dB off the top (warm), **does not compress** (crest 9.5 → 9.3–9.4 dB), costs ~+100 host instr/sample. **GROWL** is inert to ~28 (hash 8.3 %), then compresses: crest 9.5 → 8.3 (40) → 7.0 (55) → 5.5 (70),
   hash 11.8 % at 55 and 17.3 % at 70 (fuzz), costs ~+595 always on. **BIAS** (only inside GROWL) trades peak for even harmonics: at GROWL 28, BIAS 0 → 100 lifts the crest 8.3 → 10.3 dB
   (peak −8.7 → −6.1) and the 150–1.2k bands by 0.5 dB: it raises peaks, not presence. **Presence without compression = DRIVE.** G sits where I put the edge of fuzzy: hash 2× E's and crest −2.6 dB (DRIVE 110 + GROWL 55); GROWL 70 on its own is past it.
