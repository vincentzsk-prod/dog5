DØG-5

Free, open-source custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. Pick a patch, play it.

This is the real DØG-5 firmware running in your browser. It sounds like the firmware's engine; the FM-1 itself has real-time limits (its CPU budget) that a browser does not model, so a patch with everything pushed can sound different on the device.

Patches

How to play

  • Pick a patch from the list, or turn the PRESETS knob (or the ↑ ↓ keys).
  • The four knobs change whatever page the screen shows.
  • FX cycles the four effect pages (DRIVE, BBD DELAY, SPRING, TAPE); hold SEL for an effect's second set of knobs. ENV, LFO, EDIT and GLO each cycle their own family of pages. HOME goes back to the play page; hold HOME to see a map of the knobs and buttons.
  • Hold SEQ: Tape Stop (everything drags to a halt; let go to snap back). PLAY / STOP: the kill switch (stops everything dead; press again to play on). ALGORITHM knob: Vintage Slop (rides the tuning instability up; back to 0 when you reload).
  • SAVE: tap it, pick a slot with PRESETS, tap it again. It is kept in this browser only. Slots 33–99 are empty; save there to leave the 32 patches as they are.
  • REC taps the BBD delay's time, on the BBD DELAY page only.

Hold a button with the mouse and play the computer keys at the same time; on a phone, use two fingers.

Computer keyboard

The letters are printed on the keys above. Two rows, like a piano:

Keys C4 .. E5Z S X D C V G B H N J M , L . ; /
Keys F3 .. B3Q 2 W 3 E 4 R
Keys F5 .. G5I 9 O
Buttons1 HOME, 5 ENV, 7 LFO, 8 ARP, 0 SEQ, - EDIT, = GLO, [ FX, ] SAVE, Enter SEL, Space PLAY / STOP, \ REC, ← → OCT − / +
Knobs↑ ↓ PRESETS; drag a knob up / down or scroll on it

The positions are those of a US keyboard; on other layouts the same physical keys play.

MIDI keyboard

Any MIDI keyboard plays it (notes with velocity, pitch bend, mod wheel, sustain, CCs, clock), as over USB on the device.

Web MIDI works in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox; not in Safari.

Your patches

Saved patches and settings stay in this browser (localStorage). They are not shared with other browsers or people.
