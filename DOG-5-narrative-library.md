## The pivotal decision (still worth keeping): STORY vs WORLD
Is DØG-5 a plot that resolves, or a perpetual condition told in fragments? Counsel was **WORLD, with a
skeleton of story underneath** — fragmentary/atmospheric (BoC / NIN / early Bakshi), a real internal
chronology you know and never fully tell. More punk, more replayable, harder to make tacky, suits a live
instrument. This survives into the dog world as "the release is the dog getting out."

## The surviving hooks

  of the litter" (POLY 5)**. The whole 5-motif (DØG-5, Fi(l)fth, 5th quadrant) still pays off here.
- **Don't over-systematise the 32 names** — they're a found lexicon in the band's real voice (egg-punk, surf,
  skate, tech side by side), meaning implied not enforced. The scene gives the arc; the names are vocabulary.
- **The release as meta-narrative** — every artifact is "evidence of the leak" (now: evidence the dog got out).
