# Layer-lock under HOME-acts-on-release: hold FX (the layer), tap HOME -> locked. HOME's tap does NOT go HOME (its
# press was the lock, so its release does nothing); FX's release keeps the lock; the knobs stay the layer's on any page
# until FX is pressed again (or ENV / LFO / ARP), and only then does a HOME tap go HOME, on its release.
# Buttons: 2 FX, 4 ENV, 6 EDIT, 8 HOME. KNOB 1 = encoder 2: on FILTER it is RESO (13), in the FX layer GROWL (54).
# Program 1 (Red Pill): RESO 30, GROWL 0.
3000 btn 6   # EDIT: OSC 1
3200 btn 6   # OSC 2
3400 btn 6   # OSC MOD
3600 btn 6   # FILTER
3800 expect page 4
3800 expect param 13 30
3800 expect param 54 0
4000 btndown 2   # FX held: the layer
4200 btn 8   # HOME tapped while FX is held: the lock
4400 expect page 4   # (the tap's release did not go HOME)
4400 btnup 2   # FX let go: still locked
4600 expect page 4
4600 shot
4700 enc 2 1   # KNOB 1 is the layer's GROWL, not FILTER's RESO
4900 expect param 54 2
4900 expect param 13 30
5000 btn 2   # FX pressed again: the lock goes; its release does nothing (no page step)
5200 expect page 4
5200 enc 2 1   # KNOB 1 is RESO again
5400 expect param 13 32
5400 expect param 54 2
5500 btn 8   # HOME tapped with no layer: HOME, on its release
5700 expect page 0
5800 btn 6   # EDIT: back to FILTER (the family remembers)
6000 expect page 4
6000 btndown 2   # the other way out: lock again ...
6200 btn 8
6300 btnup 2
6400 expect page 4
6500 enc 2 1
6700 expect param 54 4   # locked: GROWL
6800 btn 4   # ... and ENV lets it go (without leaving the page)
7000 expect page 4
7000 enc 2 1
7200 expect param 13 34   # RESO again
7200 expect param 54 4
7300 btndown 8   # a held HOME is still the control map, not HOME
7700 expect page 4
7800 btnup 8
8000 expect page 4
8000 btn 8   # and a tap still goes HOME
8200 expect page 0
8500 end
