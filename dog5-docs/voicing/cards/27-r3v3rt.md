## 2. What it sounds like

A single-voice bass from one pulse wave. Each note opens with a bright filter snap that falls into a lower body, and the body then holds at a steady level for as long as the key is down; when the key goes up the note stops almost at once. Played legato, the snap is not re-fired and the notes after the first have no front edge. Dry.

## 3. Gear inspirations (docs only, "inspired by")

- **Roland SH-101** — *the tight, quick note; Tight, Punchy.* Key-up to −20 / −40 / −60 dB is 11 / 15 / 19 ms on A2 (measured; the 9 ms meter window is the floor of these readings). Punchy: not measured.
- **Minimoog** — *the round immediate low end; Round, Thick.* Not measured. The band shares are measured: on A2 the first 400 ms are 1 / 82 / 9 / 6 / 1 % in <60 / 60–150 / 150–400 / 400–1.2k / 1.2k+ Hz.
- **Moog Prodigy** — *the plain pulse; Raw, Fat.* A single pulse (OSC 1 PW 54, OSC 2 off); "raw" and "fat" are not measured.
- **Sequential Pro-One** — *the snap of the filter decay; Snappy.* On A2 the filter snap peaks at a centroid of 2891 Hz and lasts 175 ms (the time for the centroid to fall within 25 % of its body value); the centroid reads 2828 / 1759 / 440 Hz over 0–20 / 20–100 / 100–300 ms (measured). No comparison with the Pro-One was made.

Closest to: **an SH-101 pulse bass with a Minimoog body**.

Nothing here says the patch matches these: it was built toward the descriptions, by measurement, and approved by ear. "Round", "thick" and "warm" were part of the brief and are not measured.

## 4. How to play it

- **MONO, one note at a time.** Chords play the last key only. The filter snap fires on **detached** notes; with **legato** playing (a new key down before the old one is up) the envelopes are not re-fired. Measured on notes 2–8 of the eight-note line: the first 20 ms read −16.6 dB detached and −19.9 dB legato, with a centroid of 2200 Hz detached against 154 Hz legato (so a legato note has no snap); over 0–100 ms the level is −17.4 / −19.9 dB and the centroid in 20–100 ms is 1600 / 178 Hz.
- **Sustain and run-off (A2, held 1 s):** the level re the peak is −2.0 dB at 100 ms and −3.4 dB at 300 ms (AMP SUSTAIN 90). The decay-only filter envelope (decay 85, sustain 0) leaves the body at a centroid of 440 Hz in 100–300 ms. After key-up the level is down 20 / 40 / 60 dB in 11 / 15 / 19 ms (AMP RELEASE 30, about 5 ms).
- **Low register:** A1's fundamental is 55 Hz, so a low A1 is mostly inaudible on a small speaker. Of A1's body (50–400 ms, one 350 ms window) 17 % of the energy is above 60 Hz; on A2 it is 99 %. A1's snap centroid reads 1795 / 1317 / 305 Hz over 0–20 / 20–100 / 100–300 ms. (The 2048-point band shares smear A1's 55 Hz fundamental over 60–150 Hz: use the 17 %.)
- **Level and cost:** VOLUME 98, −18.00 dBFS RMS over the note-on region of the low chopped riff (a; K-weighted −20.1), peak −13.8 dBFS over every audition phrase (−13.9 on the riff itself); the standard stabs phrase (b) reads −18.0 dBFS, 0.0 dB from the riff. **CPU 1379 host instr/sample worst case**, against 1377 for the original: it is not lighter than the original (per-voice DRIVE 30 costs +17 on this MONO voice).
- **Dry:** no echo, no spring, no tape.
- **Tricks:** SLOBBER, SEQ held (Tape Stop), the kill switch (HEEL) are untested on this patch.
- **Changed from the brief:** the original array had a saw and a sub-octave pulse (OSC 2 SEMI −12), RESO 30, FILTER DECAY 60 with FILTER SUSTAIN 10, and AMP SUSTAIN 100. The patch now has one pulse, RESO 0, a decay-only filter envelope (decay 85, sustain 0) and AMP SUSTAIN 90; that is the sustained body Vincent asked for. The original brief is kept in `DOG-5-presets.md`.
