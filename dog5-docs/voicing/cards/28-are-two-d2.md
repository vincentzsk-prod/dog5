*Voiced 11–12 Nov 2026 (Batch 6B round 2), approved after listening: variant D2 (Vincent: "I choose D2"). **Renamed from Bell Tolls at his request** ("The previous Bell Tolls name really doesn't suit the sound anymore"); he asked for "Arr Two Dee Two", which is 15 characters against the 14-character name field, so the on-screen name is "Arr 2 Dee 2" (his choice from the options given; the font shows it in capitals). ## 2. What it sounds like

A sustained, struck bell tone built from two saw waves with a mid-frequency sideband character. It rings for several seconds, a slow shimmer moves its pitch and level, and a small echo and a spring sit behind it. It is dry of drive and tape.

## 3. Gear inspirations (docs only, "inspired by")

- **Yamaha DX7 (tubular-bells voice)** — *FM clang, inharmonic.* Partly carried: OSC 2 sits +15 semitones above OSC 1 (a ratio of 2.38) and feeds OSC 1 by X-MOD 32. At A4 in the first 300 ms that partial reads 1.197 × the prime at −11.1 dB. With the FM, 3 peaks within −30 dB sit off the key's harmonic series and off that partial (100–400 ms window, A4). Not carried: DX7-style operators (this is one saw modulating another). "Clang" is not measured.
- **Roland D-50 ("Fantasia")** — *crystalline.* **Not carried.** The engine has no per-partial envelope, so no partial outlasts the body, and the echo's band-limit darkens each repeat. At A4 the share of energy above 4 kHz is 4.0 % in the first 20 ms and 0.4 % at 1–2 s. The round-1 calibration renders Xopen and Yrise (a filter held open, a filter opening slowly) are on the branch `listen/batch-06b-bell-init`; neither was called crystalline. "Crystalline" is not carried and not measured.
- **Casio CZ-101 (phase-distortion bells)** — *a bright, bell-like phase-distortion tone.* Not carried: the engine has no phase distortion; the sideband character here comes from X-MOD. Whether it resembles those bells is not measured.
- **A struck tubular or church bell** — *the classical partials.* Carried, measured: the five classical partials of the ladder (hum 0.5, prime 1, tierce 1.2, quint 1.5, nominal 2) are all present, with the amplitudes in section 4. Other partials of a real bell were not tried. "Church bell" is Vincent's word and is not measured.

Closest to: **a DX7 tubular bell with a long, slowly moving tail**.

## 4. How to play it

- **What the key is:** the key you press is the bell's hum, the strongest partial; the prime is an octave above it. The strike may therefore feel an octave above the key (not measured as perceived pitch).
- **The bell ladder** (first 300 ms of a held note, dB re the strongest partial, with the measured ratio against the prime): at **A4** hum 0.498 at 0, prime 0.997 at −7.7, tierce 1.197 at −11.1, quint 1.496 at −10.5, nominal 1.995 at −13.6. At **A3** hum 0.499 at 0, prime 0.996 at −4.9, tierce 1.210 at −10.1, quint 1.496 at −7.8, nominal 1.994 at −12.4. At 1–2 s (A4) the same five read 0 / −9.2 / −13.7 / −15.1 / −17.4 dB.
- **Ring** (the whole patch, held 6 s, ms after the strike's peak to fall 6 / 12 / 20 / 30 / 40 dB): A4 915 / 1962 / 3094 / 4700 / 5963; A3 951 / 1668 / 3210 / 4446 / 6014. **It rings on after key-up:** A4 struck and released at 400 ms reads −0.6 / −0.5 / −0.5 dB against the same note held, 0.5 / 1 / 2 s later; the released note falls 20 dB in 3012 ms and 40 dB in 5610 ms.
- **Shimmer:** a TRI LFO at 0.37 Hz, pitch ±9.4 cents by the law, amp 20, delay 35, PHASE SYNC. On a one-voice probe the pitch wobble reads 3.25 cents s.d. (−5.3 to +4.7 cents, 0.29 Hz) and the level swing 1.67 dB.
- **Strike and body:** centroid over 0–20 / 20–100 / 100–400 ms is 3925 / 3695 / 2383 Hz at A4 and 2803 / 2413 / 1596 Hz at A3. Energy shares under 150 Hz / 150–800 / 800–4k / above 4 kHz over the strike (0–400 ms): A4 0.0 / 64.3 / 33.4 / 2.3 %, A3 0.0 / 84.0 / 15.3 / 0.7 %; over 1–2 s: A4 0.0 / 64.3 / 35.3 / 0.4 %, A3 0.0 / 86.8 / 13.2 / 0.0 %.
- **Register** (RMS over 0–300 ms of one struck note, centroid over 0–100 ms): A2 −19.8 dB / 1582 Hz, A3 −20.2 / 2568, A4 −19.9 / 3846, A5 −20.6 / 5031, A6 −19.4 / 6129. The level is within 1.2 dB across the keybed; **A6 is bright** (21.3 % of its energy in the first 20 ms is above 4 kHz; at A6 the bell's own partials sit there, not isolated from any grit). The ring to −40 dB reads 5924 ms at A2 and 5794 ms at A6.
- **Space** (A4 held 400 ms; the echo and spring return against the dry note in 0–20 / 20–100 / 100–250 / 250–1000 ms): −27.5 / −14.0 / −11.5 / −11.3 dB.
- **Chords and polyphony:** POLY 5. The three-note chord reads −21.0 dBFS RMS with a peak of −6.0 dBFS. POLY 4 / 5 / 6 against POLY 8 change the level of what rings by at worst −0.92 / −0.24 / 0.0 dB on the slow toll and −1.04 / −0.77 / −0.97 dB on the fast run's tail (a small cut ring or the random start phases, not isolated); the chord is unchanged.
- **Fast run:** eight notes read −15.8 dBFS RMS with a peak of −4.03 dBFS, no clip. That run, not the chord, sets the peak limit on the level.
- **What round 2 did, and did not do** (measured on D2 against round 1's D): the top grit was the FM, not CHEW, GROWL or drive (none of them is in the patch). X-MOD 64 → 32 lowers the roughness over 2–10 kHz (spectral flatness at A4, 100–400 ms, −16.9 → −25.4 dB; A3 −12.1 → −18.5 dB) and the number of peaks within −30 dB that are off the harmonic series (12 → 3 at A4, 100–400 ms). It does **not** lower the share above 4 kHz (A4 0–20 / 20–100 / 100–400 ms / 1–2 s: 4.59 / 4.36 / 1.89 / 0.50 % → 4.04 / 4.39 / 1.61 / 0.44 %); that share is the saw's harmonics through the open filter at the strike (suggests, from the isolation set: it moves with CUTOFF and the filter envelope, not with X-MOD). The original array had a harmonic interval (OSC 2 +19 st), no sideband floor and no inharmonic tierce (−47 dB at A4); the tierce here is at −11.1 dB.
- **Which of Vincent's wishes it carries:** carried, measured: the tail lengths as round 1's D (ring to −40 dB 5963 ms against 5979), the bell ladder underneath (D2 within 2.1 dB of D at A4), the spring at DRAIN 35 and the echo as in D, no CHEW, GROWL or drive, and a lower 2–10 kHz roughness. Not carried: a crystal tail (above) and a lower share above 4 kHz. "Crisper", "cleaner", "dreamy", "body", "personality" and "a clear church bell" are Vincent's words, not measured; only the roughness numbers above moved.
- **Known small misses against round 1's D:** the −12 dB ring point is 1962 ms against 1895 (+3.5 %); the echo and spring return in 100–250 ms is +1.4 dB against round 1; the toll level is −19.13 dBFS RMS on the slow toll, inside the −18.0 ± 1.5 band and 0.37 dB from its lower edge (VOLUME 105 is the highest value found by search whose peak over every phrase tried stays at −4.0 dBFS or lower; the fast run is the phrase at the limit).
- **Live shaping:** the base sits mid-range on the controls Vincent will reach (CUTOFF 88, X-MOD 32, ENV>O2 64, OSC 2 FINE 64, echo MIX 45, DRAIN 35, CHEW 0). One step each way at A3, centroid over 0–100 ms (base 2568 Hz), ring to −20 / −40 dB (base 3210 / 6014 ms):

  - **X-MOD 32 −20 / +20:** 2443 / 2565 Hz; ring unchanged within 8 ms; 3 off-harmonic partials above −25 dB at both ends.
  - **ENV>O2 64 −10 / +10 (the one that breaks the bell):** the tierce falls from −10 dB to −47 dB at −10 and −49 dB at +10 (the nearest peak reads 1.165 at +10); the other four partials move by at most 1 dB.
  - **OSC 2 FINE 64 −8 / +8:** the tierce ratio reads 1.208 / 1.192 against 1.210; centroid 2558 / 2531 Hz.
  - **Echo MIX 45 −15 / +15:** no change to the partials; ring to −20 dB 3206 / 3233 against 3210.
  - **DRAIN 35 −15 / +15:** no change to the partials (tierce 1.211 / 1.208); centroid 2555 / 2580 Hz.
  - **CHEW 0 → 45** (it has no way down): prime and nominal move to 0.994 / 2.006, centroid 2360 Hz, 6 off-harmonic partials above −25 dB (3 at base).
- **CPU** (host instr/sample, worst case): base 2118 (idle 1178, load 939); **base + CHEW 45 2901, base + GROWL 44 2713, both 3496 (all three over the 2400 flag)**. The device render time is not measured.
- **Level and peak:** −19.13 dBFS RMS on the slow toll, peak over all phrases tried −4.03 dBFS, no sample at full scale; the dry strike's crest factor is 10.5 dB.
- **Tricks:** SLOBBER, SEQ held (Tape Stop), the kill switch (HEEL): untested on this patch.
