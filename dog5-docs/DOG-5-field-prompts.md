# DØG-5 — GEMINI IMAGE PROMPTS (the four fields + Le Gato props)

**Status:** 27 Oct 2026. Prompts for the four scene backgrounds and the Le Gato props, for a Gemini image
bot (raw samples already in Gemini, working from them there). Two things baked in beyond the character brief:
**(1)** these are pure black-and-white (scene colour is added later in firmware as the moving beam, NOT
painted into the background); **(2)** for Le Gato the bin and the cat are pulled OUT of the field and made
into separate prop images, because they animate (two states each) — the field is just alley + wall + washing
line with empty gaps where they drop in.

Delivery for all: black ink on white, line + solid only (no grey), drawn large (~4×, work ≥600px for a figure
that lands ~140–150px), one element per sheet/layer, heavy black keyline. 1:1 square, reduces to 240×240 1-bit.

---

**SHARED RULES — applies to every image (paste first, or glue to each)**
```
Style: pure black and white only. No colour, no grey, no gradients, no soft
shading, no anti-aliasing. Shading only as 1-bit dither or halftone dots.
Every shape has a bold, heavy black outline. Hand-inked DIY-punk look:
confident, slightly wonky marker-and-ink linework, vintage photocopied /
risograph grime, very high contrast. Flat and head-on — no 3D, no
photorealism, no perspective tricks. Square 1:1, will be reduced to a
240×240 pixel screen, so keep it bold and simple.
```

**FIELD I — JALOPY**
```
A background field. Keep the LEFT THIRD empty/dark and keep a clear empty
diagonal lane running from the left up to the TOP-RIGHT corner (a bright
beam will cross there later). Put the main shape low and to the right.
Subject: the back end of a beat-up car driving out of the right edge of
frame — you see its rear: bumper, two tail-lights, an exhaust pipe puffing
smoke, one wheel. A hot tar road runs along the bottom with a raised kerb
line. Derelict, scorched Cape Flats roadside. Sparse white dither flecks for
heat and dust. Dark empty sky above. The car's rear silhouette is the one
hero shape.
```

**FIELD II — BEE-TCH**
```
A background field. Keep the LEFT THIRD empty/dark and keep a clear empty
diagonal lane up to the TOP-RIGHT corner. Subject: a dead-flat, cold sea
horizon — ONE strong horizontal line sitting low in the frame (around the
lower third), with a low band of dithered sea beneath it. Rising out of the
sea on the RIGHT, a clump of bull-kelp (a few bulbous stalks). Everything
above the horizon is near-empty cold dark sky. Bleak, flat, cold-water Cape
beach. The flat horizon line plus the kelp clump are the hero shapes.
```

**FIELD III — MOONSHINE**
```
A background field. Keep the LEFT THIRD empty/dark and keep the TOP-RIGHT
corner and a clear lane up to it empty. Subject: a bleak empty night, almost
entirely solid black. ONE clean solid WHITE disc — a full moon — sitting
high in the frame (upper-centre, NOT in the top-right corner), the single
brightest thing in the picture. A faint far-horizon line low near the
bottom. No stars, no clutter — maybe one or two dither specks. Lonely and
cold. The white moon disc is the only hero shape.
```

**FIELD IV — LE GATO (field only — no bin, no cat)**
```
A background field. Keep the LEFT THIRD empty/dark and keep a clear empty
diagonal lane up to the TOP-RIGHT corner. Subject: a Cape Town back alley at
dusk. A low brick-and-plaster boundary wall running across the frame. A
washing line strung across the upper area with a couple of hanging items (a
shirt, a sock). Alley ground along the bottom. IMPORTANT: leave an empty
flat patch of ground on the RIGHT for a bin, and an empty clear stretch
along the TOP OF THE WALL for a cat — do NOT draw a bin and do NOT draw a
cat. The wall and washing line are the hero shapes.
```

**PROP — WHEELIE BIN (two separate images, isolated on plain white, object only, centred, same style)**
```
A) A standard two-wheel wheelie bin standing upright, lid closed, seen from
a slight front angle, bold black outline, 1-bit black-and-white, isolated on
plain white.

B) The SAME wheelie bin knocked over on its side, lid flung open, a little
rubbish spilling out. Same size and style as A so they swap cleanly.
```

**PROP — CAT (two separate images, solid black silhouette, isolated on plain white)**
```
A) A cat sitting calmly in side-on silhouette, tail curled around its feet,
totally unbothered. Solid black shape, clearly reads as a cat.

B) The SAME cat the instant it bolts — back arched, fur spiked, mid-scramble
leaping away in fright. Same silhouette style as A so they swap cleanly.
```
